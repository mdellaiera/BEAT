1 · Measure

Each heartbeat changes how much blood is in the skin, and so its colour, by a fraction of a percent. BEAT splits the frame into small cells, turns each cell's colour into a pulse candidate, and weights the cells by how well they agree with each other.

2 · Magnify

MIT 2012 is Eulerian Video Magnification as published: amplify everything inside a hand-picked frequency band. BEAT amplifies only what follows the measured pulse, so no band has to be chosen and the background stays still.

3 · Check

Three published estimators (GREEN, CHROM, POS) run alongside on the same clip. This is a demonstration, not a medical device.

Demo clip and reference result from Wu, Rubinstein, Shih, Guttag, Durand and Freeman, Eulerian Video Magnification for Revealing Subtle Changes in the World, SIGGRAPH 2012.
